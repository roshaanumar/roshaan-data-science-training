Company Compliance Policy

Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is a process used for customers who present a higher level of risk. It involves collecting additional information about the customer, understanding the source of funds, reviewing business activities, and conducting additional monitoring.

EDD may be required for high-risk customers, politically exposed persons, customers from high-risk jurisdictions, or customers involved in complex transactions.

Customer Due Diligence

Customer Due Diligence (CDD) is the process of identifying and verifying customers before establishing a business relationship. It includes collecting customer information and assessing the customer's risk level.

Risk Assessment

Customers are categorized as low, medium, or high risk. High-risk customers require additional monitoring and may require Enhanced Due Diligence.

Transaction Monitoring

The company monitors customer transactions to identify unusual or suspicious activity. Transactions that meet certain risk criteria may be reviewed by the compliance team.
